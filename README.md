# My Favorite Movie: Gran Turismo (2023)

**Genre:** Action / Sports / Drama  
*Director:* Neill Blomkamp  

### Main Cast
1. Archie Madekwe as Jann Mardenborough  
2. David Harbour as Jack Salter  
3. Orlando Bloom as Danny Moore  
4. Darren Barnet as Matty Davis  

## Plot Summary
**Gran Turismo (2023)** is based on the true story of *Jann Mardenborough*, a gamer who becomes a professional race car driver.  
After proving his skills in the **Gran Turismo video game**, he is selected to compete in real-life racing through the *GT Academy* program.  
The movie shows his inspiring journey from virtual racing to professional motorsports.

### My Favorite Scenes in the movie
- The intense final race at Le Mans   
- When Jann overcomes fear after the crash   
- The training montage showing his determination to learn and win   

### My review of the movie
I really enjoyed this movie because it shows how **passion, dedication, and dreams** can turn into reality.  
It’s a mix of *inspiration*, *adrenaline*, and *emotion* that motivates anyone who watches it.

In summary, *Gran Turismo (2023)* is not just a racing movie — it’s about never giving up on your dreams!
